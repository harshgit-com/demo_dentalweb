Your everyday basics

Teeth are not just about a smile.

They help you eat, speak and protect the structures around them. Good oral hygiene is one of the easiest long-term health habits to build.

Brush twice a day

Use a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste for around two minutes.

Clean between teeth

Floss or use an interdental cleaner to reach areas your brush cannot.

Don’t ignore bleeding

Frequent bleeding while brushing can be a sign your gums need attention.

Keep regular visits

Your dentist can tailor the right check-up and cleaning interval for you.
